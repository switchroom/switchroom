WHEN AN AGENT DIES MID-TASK

Things die. Switchroom brings it back, and it picks up where it left off.

A crash or a forced stop in the middle of your request does not lose the work. The agent restarts on its own, is handed the unfinished turn, and continues, so nothing gets silently dropped.
